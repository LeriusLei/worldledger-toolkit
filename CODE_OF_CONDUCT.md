# Community conduct

Discuss evidence and code with care. Be respectful, give actionable criticism, credit sources and distinguish observed results from interpretation. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks and publishing another person's private information are not welcome.

Maintain reproducibility: report failures and missing outcomes honestly, avoid fabricated measurements and preserve the conditions attached to scientific claims. Never post credentials or unredacted private logs in issues or pull requests.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and limit participation when necessary. To raise a conduct concern, request a private contact channel without disclosing personal or sensitive details in a public issue.
